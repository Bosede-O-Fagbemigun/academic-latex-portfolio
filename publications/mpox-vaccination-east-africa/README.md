## Research Problem

The study investigates mpox transmission dynamics in East Africa and
examines the potential impact of vaccination strategies on disease
transmission and control.

The mathematical modelling framework provides a quantitative basis for
comparing vaccination interventions and understanding their implications
for disease control.

## Modelling Focus

The research uses mathematical modelling to describe disease transmission
and evaluate intervention strategies.

Particular emphasis is placed on vaccination as a control measure and on
regional differences in transmission and intervention outcomes.
