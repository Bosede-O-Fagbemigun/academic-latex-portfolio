## Research Problem

The study investigates applications of F_h-convex functions to integral
inequalities and economic models on time scales.

The time-scale framework provides a unified setting for continuous and
discrete analysis, allowing mathematical results to be considered across
different underlying time domains.

## Mathematical Focus

The work combines generalized convexity with time-scale calculus to develop
and apply integral inequalities in an economic context.

Particular attention is given to the relationship between generalized
convexity structures, integral inequalities, and economic applications.
