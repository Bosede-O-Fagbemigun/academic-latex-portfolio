## Research Problem

The study compares Ordinary Least Squares (OLS), Ridge regression, and
LASSO regression as approaches for mitigating the effects of
multicollinearity in linear models.

The comparison examines how regularization-based methods can improve
the stability of regression models when predictor variables are highly
correlated.

## Methodological Focus

The work considers three regression approaches:

- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
- Ridge Regression
- Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator (LASSO)

Ridge and LASSO introduce regularization to address limitations that may
arise in ordinary least-squares estimation under multicollinearity.
