# Microarray Survival Analysis Using BART

## Publication

**Okolie, F. A., Fagbemigun, B. O. & Samson, O. J. (2025).**

*Microarray Survival Analysis of Five Cancer Gene Sequences and its
Application using Bayesian Additive Regression Trees (BART).*

Sri Lankan Journal of Applied Statistics, 26(3), 120–148.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.4038/sljas.v26i3.8162

## Research Problem

The study investigates survival-related patterns in cancer gene-expression
data and examines the application of Bayesian Additive Regression Trees
(BART) to survival analysis.

## Methodological Focus

The research combines statistical survival-analysis techniques with
Bayesian machine-learning methodology to investigate relationships between
gene-expression variables and survival outcomes.

## Data

The study uses microarray cancer gene-expression data involving five cancer
gene sequences.

## Key Method

### Bayesian Additive Regression Trees (BART)

BART provides a flexible Bayesian approach for modelling potentially
nonlinear relationships between predictors and survival-related outcomes.
