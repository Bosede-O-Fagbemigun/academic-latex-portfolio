## Research Problem

The work investigates generalized Hermite-Hadamard-Mercer type inequalities
and related estimates for multiplicative convex functions.

The study develops inequalities that extend classical Hermite-Hadamard-type
relationships and examines their associated estimates and applications.

## Mathematical Focus

The research is situated within the broader theory of convexity and integral
inequalities, with particular emphasis on generalized forms of
Hermite-Hadamard inequalities.
